About the practice

A small practice, with time for each person.

Snehpushp — “the flower of affection” — is a personal Ayurvedic consultation practice. We believe good guidance begins with attention: a careful conversation, a thorough examination and a clear explanation of what we observe.

Our Vaidyas are B.A.M.S. qualified Ayurvedic physicians. Every recommendation is shaped by your constitution, your current state and the realities of your everyday life.

“Ayurveda does not look at a condition alone. It looks at the person living with it.”

Ayurvedic consultation

What happens when you visit.

A consultation is a conversation and an assessment — not a quick prescription. Here is what you can expect.

  1. 01

    Your history

    We begin by listening — present concerns, past illnesses, medicines you take and how things have changed over time.

  2. 02

    Diet, routine & sleep

    What you eat, when you wake and sleep, your work and stress — the everyday patterns Ayurveda pays close attention to.

  3. 03

    Clinical examination

    A careful examination, including Nadi Pariksha (pulse assessment) where appropriate, alongside any relevant observations.

  4. 04

    Personalised guidance

    Recommendations explained clearly — Ayurvedic medicine where suitable, with diet and routine guidance you can follow.

  5. 05

    Follow-up

    Progress is reviewed over time. Please bring previous reports and prescriptions to your follow-up visits.

Online consultations

Can’t visit in person? Online consultations are available. Prior appointment is required for all consultations.

Our approach

Classical principles. Individual assessment.

  • Shastrokta — grounded in the classics

    Our reasoning follows the principles of classical Ayurvedic texts, applied thoughtfully to present-day lives.

  • You, not a template

    Two people with the same complaint may need different guidance. Assessment always comes before advice.

  • Honest and restrained

    We explain what Ayurveda can offer in your situation, and we do not promise outcomes. Response differs from person to person.

  • Alongside your daily life

    Guidance is practical — fitted to your food, work and sleep, so it can actually be followed.

Information on this website is general and is not a substitute for a personal consultation. In an emergency, please contact your nearest hospital.
